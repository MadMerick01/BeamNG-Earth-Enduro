Tips
  • Copy the coordinates on the Route tab, paste them into the map search and teleport there to start or resume.
  • You earn $100 for every kilometre you drive. Teleports earn nothing.
  • Your sponsor pays a bonus for every stretch of driving: the bar under your money shows how far to the next one. Teleports do not count.
  • Save often. Money, mileage and parts since your last Save are lost when you exit or load the level again.
  • Ironman: if your car is wrecked and you cannot pay for the repair, it is game over and the stage starts again.
  • Repair damage before it becomes a wreck. A wreck's repair costs at least $5,100.
  • Parts taken off are traded in for 30% of their value. Trying parts on and swapping back is free.
  • Driving more than 25 m with an unpaid parts or car bill puts your paid-for car back.
  • Beat the clock: the time limit counts down while you drive and stops while the game is paused. Every repair, and every new car, costs you 5 minutes. Buying parts costs no time.
  • Pace compares your time with how far you are, in a straight line, from start to finish. Winding roads can put you behind early on and ahead later.
  • Missing the time limit is not game over: the clock keeps counting in red as overtime.
